# ESCORE — Brand guide
Independent Free Fire esports media, MENA / Global.

Colors: Night #090B10, Panel #121722, Signal Lime #C8FF32, White #F5F7FA, Muted #A8B0BF.
Typography: Barlow Condensed for editorial headlines; Inter for interface; SVG uses Arial for portability.
Use the logo on dark backgrounds, with clear space at least the height of the mark's first horizontal bar. Do not stretch or recolor arbitrarily.
Post templates are editable SVGs: replace the placeholder photo, headline, source and date with verified information and authorized imagery. Export to PNG for social media.
Tagline: Every match. Every story.
No Garena, FFWS or EWC affiliation is implied. Check trademark availability before public launch.
